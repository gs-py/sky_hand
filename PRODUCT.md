## Platform

## Stack

delegated: React + Vite + Tailwind CSS + Motion, requested by the user.

## Users

Businesses importing, exporting, and distributing cargo through Hong Kong, Mainland China, and international markets. They need to explain a shipment quickly and request a coordinated logistics solution.

## Product Purpose

SKY HANDLERS LOGISTICS LIMITED Hong Kong provides freight forwarding and logistics coordination across air freight, sea freight, local transportation, warehousing, customs clearance, dangerous goods, and cargo consolidation.

## Positioning

One logistics partner coordinating freight, clearance, transportation, warehousing, and distribution across Hong Kong, Mainland China, and a wider international network.

## Operating Context

Shipments may originate from factories and suppliers in China, move through Hong Kong, and continue by air or sea to international destinations. Customers may need time-critical freight, consolidation, cross-border transport, customs support, or specialized handling.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Air freight, sea freight, Hong Kong and China logistics, local transportation, cross-border logistics, warehousing, customs clearance, dangerous goods support, and cargo consolidation. Hong Kong office address, telephone, email, WhatsApp, and office hours are not supplied and must remain placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name SKY HANDLERS LOGISTICS LIMITED Hong Kong and the core message “Your Logistics Gateway to Hong Kong, China & the World.” The requested experience is professional and non-generic.

- Connect Hong Kong and Mainland China capability to international reach.
